WARCAMP

Gather. Build. Conquer. 1–4 players.

To install: tap Share, then “Add to Home Screen”.

Game setup

How to play

  • Goal: destroy every enemy building. You lose when your last building falls.
  • Workers gather crystal (◆) from crystals and trees and carry it to your Keep. Select workers to build.
  • Cottages raise your unit limit. Barracks train Soldiers and Archers. A Stable (needs a Barracks) trains Riders. Watchtowers shoot.
  • Your Champion is your strongest unit, has a Shockwave attack and comes back at the Keep 30 seconds after falling.

Touch

  • Tap a unit or building to select it. Double-tap a unit to select all of its kind on screen.
  • With units selected, tap the ground to move, an enemy to attack, a crystal or tree to gather.
  • Drag to scroll, pinch to zoom. Hold then drag (or tap ▭ first) to select with a box.
  • ⚔ selects your whole army. ⛏ jumps to an idle worker. Tap the map in the corner to jump there.

Mouse and keyboard

  • Left-click or drag a box to select, right-click to give orders, wheel to zoom.
  • Arrow keys or WASD scroll. Q army, . idle worker, Esc cancel.

Multiplayer

  • One player hosts and shares the 4-letter code; the others type it and tap Join. Empty seats can be computers.
  • If a player drops out, a computer takes over their base.
