HOW TO MAKE AN AIRCRAFT FOR SKYLARK

Every aircraft is one small text file ending in .json. To add your own:

1. In the Files app go to On My iPhone > Skylark.
2. Copy a file from the Examples folder into the Aircraft folder.
3. Rename it, open it in any text editor, and change the "id" to something new.
   (Keep a built-in id instead and your file replaces that aircraft.)
4. Change whatever you like, save, then in the game open Hangar and tap Reload aircraft.

If the file has a mistake, the Hangar tells you what and where.


THE SETTINGS

"id"           A short name nothing else uses, like "my-glider".
"name"         What the Hangar calls it.
"type"         How it flies: "plane" or "helicopter".
"description"  One line for the Hangar. Optional.
"cockpit"      Where the pilot's eyes are, for the cockpit view: [x, y, z]. Optional.


HOW IT FLIES: "flight"

All of these are optional.

For a plane:
  "stallSpeed"  Knots. Below this the wing stops flying. It is also roughly the
                speed it lifts off and lands at. A trainer is about 45, a jet 95.
  "topSpeed"    Knots. The fastest it goes level at full throttle.
  "power"       Engine thrust as a share of the weight. 0.3 is a light plane,
                0.8 a fighter. More power climbs steeper and takes off shorter.
  "rollRate"    Degrees a second it rolls with the stick fully over. 60 is lazy, 180 is sharp.
  "pitchRate"   Degrees a second the nose comes up with the stick fully back. About 40.
  "yawRate"     Degrees a second from full rudder. About 20.

For a helicopter:
  "topSpeed"    Knots. The fastest it goes leaning fully forward.
  "power"       The most the rotor can lift, as a multiple of the weight.
                1.5 hovers at two thirds lift; it must be above 1.15.
  "agility"     How briskly it leans where the stick points. 1 is normal.
  "yawRate"     Degrees a second it turns on the spot with full rudder. About 60.


BUILDING IT: "parts"

An aircraft is built from a list of solid shapes.

Everything is measured in metres from the middle of the aircraft:
  x goes forward (the nose), y goes up, z goes out along the right wing.
A light plane is about 8 long with wings reaching 5 out each side.
The lowest part is taken to be the wheels: that is what touches the ground.

Every part has:
  "shape"     "box", "sphere", "cylinder", "cone", "wing" or "fin"
  "size"      [length, height, width]: how big it is along x, y and z
  "color"     like "#E94F37". Two more digits make it see-through: "#BFE9FFCC" is glass.
  "position"  [x, y, z] of its middle (for a wing or fin, of its root). Optional.
  "rotation"  [x, y, z] degrees to turn it about each of those axes. Optional.
  "mirror"    true makes a second copy on the other side of the aircraft:
              describe the right wing or wheel, and the left one comes free.
  "spin"      makes it turn while the engine runs:
                "propeller"  about the line through nose and tail
                "rotor"      about the upright line, like a main rotor
                "tailrotor"  about the line through the wings

The shapes:
  box        A block. "radius" rounds its edges.
  sphere     A ball, squashed or stretched to the size: good for cabins and canopies.
  cylinder   A tube lying nose to tail. "taper" narrows its front end: 1 is none,
             0.5 is half as wide at the front. To narrow the back instead, as on a
             tail, turn it round with "rotation": [0, 180, 0].
  cone       A cylinder that comes to a point at the front.
  wing       A flat surface reaching out to the right from its root, by size[2].
             "taper" narrows the tip (0.5 is a tip half as wide as the root),
             "sweep" moves the tip back by that many metres,
             "dihedral" raises the tip by that many metres.
  fin        The same, but standing up from its root, by size[1]. Takes "taper" and "sweep".


A TINY EXAMPLE

{
  "id": "paper-dart",
  "name": "Paper Dart",
  "type": "plane",
  "flight": { "stallSpeed": 30, "topSpeed": 110, "power": 0.3, "rollRate": 140 },
  "parts": [
    { "shape": "cone", "position": [0, 0, 0], "size": [8, 0.5, 0.6], "color": "#FFFFFF" },
    { "shape": "wing", "position": [-1.5, 0, 0], "size": [5, 0.06, 3.2], "taper": 0.1, "sweep": 2.4,
      "mirror": true, "color": "#DDE3EA" },
    { "shape": "fin", "position": [-2.5, 0, 0], "size": [2.5, 0.9, 0.06], "taper": 0.2, "sweep": 1.2,
      "rotation": [180, 0, 0], "color": "#C9D1D9" }
  ]
}
